# Bharathashetra School of Dance — Parent Portal

Check payments, attendance, class times, photos, and announcements.

**Website:** https://portal.bharathashetra.org

---

## Signing in

- **Email:** the email address on file with the school
- **PIN:** your 4-digit code (the last 4 digits of your phone number unless you were given a different one)

If you have more than one dancer, **one login shows all of them**.

Forgot your PIN? Contact the school and we'll reset it.

---

## Add it to your phone's home screen

It then opens like a regular app — full screen, no address bar.

### iPhone / iPad
1. Open the website in **Safari** (this does not work in Chrome on iPhone)
2. Tap the **Share** button — the square with an arrow pointing up
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Tap **Add**

### Android
1. Open the website in **Chrome**
2. Tap the **⋮** menu in the top-right
3. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**
4. Tap **Install**

### Computer (Chrome or Edge)
Click the **install icon** in the address bar, or open the **⋮** menu and choose **Install**.

---

## What you can do

| Tab | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Each dancer's class day and time, attendance rate, what's due |
| **Payments** | Balance owed, Zelle details, payment history |
| **Attendance** | Each dancer's record separately, with dates |
| **Photos** | Recital and event albums |
| **Contact** | Message the school directly |
| **Settings** | Change your PIN |

---

## Notes

- **Change your PIN** after your first sign-in: **Settings → Change Your PIN**.
- Payment is **$80 per dancer per month**, due by the **10th**. Two dancers is $160.
