Our School

A Tradition, Carefully Kept

Bharathashetra School of Dance is a community of students and families devoted to the classical Indian dance form of Bharatanatyam.

We teach the art as it has been passed down — with patience, precision, and deep respect for its origins. Students begin with the fundamentals of posture, rhythm, and expression, and grow into confident performers who understand not only the steps but the stories and devotion behind them.

Classes are small enough that every dancer is seen. Progress is measured in years, not weeks, and we take pride in the discipline that produces. We welcome beginners with no prior experience as warmly as we welcome dancers continuing a lifelong practice.

The Art Form

What Is Bharatanatyam?

One of the oldest classical dance forms of India, born in the temples of Tamil Nadu — a union of rhythm, storytelling, and devotion.

I

Nritta

Pure rhythmic movement. Precise footwork and geometry performed for the beauty of the form itself — the discipline every dancer stands on.

II

Nritya

Expressive dance. Hand gestures and facial expression bring poetry and scripture to life, teaching students to tell a story without a single word.

III

Natya

Dramatic storytelling. The dancer becomes each character in turn, carrying epics and devotional tales to the audience through movement alone.

“Where the hand goes, the eyes follow. Where the eyes go, the mind follows. Where the mind goes, the expression follows.”

Nandikeshvara · Abhinaya Darpana

The Journey

The Path to Arangetram

The arangetram — literally "ascending the stage" — is a dancer's formal solo debut, and the milestone years of training build toward. It is not an ending but a beginning: the moment a student is recognized as ready to carry the tradition forward on their own.

Not every student pursues one, and there is no obligation to. For those who do, we prepare them thoroughly — technically, musically, and in understanding the meaning behind every piece they perform.

Foundation

Adavus, posture, and rhythm. The vocabulary of the form, practiced until it becomes instinct.

Expression

Abhinaya — conveying emotion and story through the eyes, hands, and face.

Repertoire

Learning the traditional sequence of pieces, from alarippu through thillana.

Arangetram

A full solo performance with live musicians, before family, teachers, and community.

Before You Ask

Common Questions

What age can my child start?
Most students begin between ages five and seven, when they can hold focus for a full class and follow rhythmic instruction. That said, we have started dancers both younger and considerably older — get in touch and we'll advise based on the individual child.
Does my child need previous experience?
Not at all. Most of our students begin with none. Beginners are grouped together and taught from the very first position, so nobody is trying to catch up with dancers who started earlier.
Can adults learn Bharatanatyam?
Yes. Adult beginners are welcome, including parents who danced as children and want to return to it. Progress looks different but the form rewards patience at any age.
What should we wear to class?
For regular classes, comfortable clothing that allows free movement — typically a salwar kameez or leggings with a fitted top. Dance is performed barefoot. Costumes and ankle bells are only needed for performances, and we'll guide you well in advance.
How often are classes, and how long until my child performs?
Contact us for current class days and times at each studio. Students typically take part in a group recital within their first year or two; solo performance comes considerably later, after the foundations are secure.
Which studio should we attend?
We teach in Flower Mound and Keller. Most families choose whichever is closer — mention your preference when you get in touch and we'll let you know what's available there.
What does it cost?
Tuition is monthly, with separate fees for recital costumes and events. Please contact us for current rates — we're happy to walk through what a year looks like.
